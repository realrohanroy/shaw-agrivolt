Why We Do It

The Challenge

Every year, millions of tons of organic waste end up in landfills or are left unmanaged, releasing harmful methane emissions and missing the opportunity to be recovered as a valuable resource.

At the same time, global regulatory pressures and the accelerating impacts of climate change are driving demand for a more diversified and resilient energy landscape.

Our Solution

Vanguard Renewables delivers a scalable, integrated solution to address this dual challenge that: 

  • Diverts organic waste from landfills and incineration 
  • Produces pipeline-ready renewable natural gas 
  • Supports domestic energy infrastructure 
  • Offsets carbon emissions at scale
