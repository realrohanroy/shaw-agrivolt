The industrial case

Agricultural waste can supply 2.5× India’s annual natural gas demand

Policy mandates: 20% CBG blending in CNG by 2028 — a guaranteed demand of 8 MMT annually.

Diesel replacement economics: Bio-CNG at ₹45–55/kg vs. diesel at ₹90+/litre — a 40–50% fuel cost advantage.

Soil depletion: Indian agricultural soil organic carbon at 0.3–0.5% — organic fertiliser restores it to the target 1.0%.

Sources: SATAT scheme (MoPNG), Indian Biogas Association, ICAR soil health data, 2024.
